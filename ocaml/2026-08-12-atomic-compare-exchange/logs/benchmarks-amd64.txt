amd64 benchmarks. Baseline is atomic-cas-trunk (#14980 as it stands),
branch is the same tree with compare-exchange as the primitive. Both
compilers built from the same checkout on the same machine.

Three harnesses, because the first two disagreed and I would rather show
that than pick the flattering one. The short version is that the branch
is stable at about 0.45 ns for the discarded-boolean shape in all three,
while the baseline swings between 0.37 and 0.48 depending on harness, so
that shape is layout luck rather than a real difference. The shape that
actually changed is the one where the boolean feeds a branch.

100M iters, 9 reps, min and median. This is the harness the reviewers
asked for on #14575. It is also the one where the baseline gets lucky.
