Compare-and-set codegen, before and after making compare-exchange the primitive.

Source (src/cas_shapes.ml):

    let r = Atomic.make 0
    
    (* boolean consumed by a branch, the common shape *)
    let branchy () = if Atomic.compare_and_set r 1 2 then 10 else 20
    
    (* boolean materialised as a value *)
    let valued () = Atomic.compare_and_set r 3 4

branchy() is the shape real code uses, where the boolean feeds a branch.
Assembler directives, CFI and the arm64 instruction-length assertions are
stripped for readability. Full unedited output is in asm/.
